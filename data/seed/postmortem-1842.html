Postmortem INC-1842

The March payment delay was caused by the ledger-service v2.4.1 deploy on 2 March 2024, which cut retries from 5 to 1.

Affected services were checkout-api, settlement-worker, and merchant-notifier.

Mitigation was a rollback of ledger-service to v2.3.9 at 2024-03-06 11:40 UTC.

Documents that confirm the timeline are the change log, this postmortem, and the SRE timeline.

The postmortem does not contain a dollar estimate of lost revenue.
